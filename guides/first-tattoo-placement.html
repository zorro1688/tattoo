Six questions that narrow the choice

  • Decide your visibility preference for daily life.
  • Notice how the design follows body flow.
  • Leave room for future tattoo space if you may add more later.
  • Consider clothing coverage in the situations that matter to you.
  • Check design orientation from your own view and another person’s view.
  • Bring your options to an artist consultation for practical feedback.
