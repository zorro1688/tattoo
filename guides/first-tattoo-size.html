Think of a size range as creative reference material for a qualified tattoo artist, who can help fit the design to the body area.

Six ways to choose a useful range

  • Set a readable-detail threshold for the concept you want.
  • Match the design to the available placement area.
  • Allow enough space for the lineweight your concept needs.
  • Consider how the design reads from a comfortable viewing distance.
  • Leave room for future expansion if that matters to you.
  • Ask for an artist sizing check before committing to dimensions.
