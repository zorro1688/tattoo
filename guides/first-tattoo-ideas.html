first tattoo ideas

Start with a direction that feels like you, then use it as creative reference material for a conversation with a qualified tattoo artist.

Six directions to explore

  • Personal symbols that hold a specific memory or value.
  • Nature motifs such as a leaf, wave, or landscape detail.
  • Animals chosen for their character or connection.
  • Objects and hobbies that reflect everyday interests.
  • Lettering with a word, date, or short phrase you can live with.
  • Abstract geometry built from balance, repetition, or contrast.
